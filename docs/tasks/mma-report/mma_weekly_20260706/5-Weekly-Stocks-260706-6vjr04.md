GEOCOSMIC CRITICAL REVERSAL DATES (CRDs)  

These dates affect all markets. They are the midpoints of geocosmic clusters and have a normal orb 
of three days on either side (82% rate of frequency). Sometimes, they expand to as much as six days (90+% 
frequency with that orb). The idea is to see a new two-week or greater high or low and then a reversal. It 
is  especially  effective  when  major,  half-primary,  or  primary  cycle  troughs  are  due.  These  are  more 
important than the solar-lunar reversal dates: the more stars, the greater the historical correlation with a 
cycle culmination. Please read Volume 3 of the Stock Market Timing series for more information. Below 
is the midpoint date, and in parentheses, the length of time containing the geocosmic signatures (known 
as a "cluster"). If the cluster is long (more than 15 days), there may be other possible reversals based on 
tighter geocosmic clusters within the greater cluster.
